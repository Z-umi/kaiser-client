Kaiser Launcher

The Kaiser Launcher is in development. It will set everything up for you and start the game with Kaiser.

Sign in

Log in with your own Microsoft account, the same one you use for Minecraft.

Automatic setup

The launcher downloads the official game files from Mojang and installs Fabric and Kaiser Client in their own folder.

Always up to date

New Kaiser versions are installed automatically when you start the launcher.

Play

Press Play and the game starts with Kaiser. Your normal Minecraft stays untouched.

Privacy

The Kaiser Launcher uses the Microsoft login only to start Minecraft with your own account.

  • You sign in on Microsoft's own sign-in page. The launcher never sees your password.
  • The login tokens are only stored on your own computer, so you stay signed in. They are never sent to Kaiser servers or anyone else.
  • Your account is only used to talk to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang to start the game.
  • Kaiser Client is free. It sells no gameplay advantages.
  • You can sign out in the launcher at any time; this removes the saved login from your computer.
